# Youtopia artwork

Four original pixel-art scene assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this application. All values, tax bands, calculations, labels, controls and economic outcomes remain native text/code. Artwork is decorative scenery, not a source of economic data.

- `dist/assets/youtopia-map.webp`: a 16-bit island terrain, ocean, forest, farms and river, using the greybox silhouette as the geographic reference and leaving clearings for live buildings. The treasury, homes, worksites, lighthouse, beam, road disruption and labels are rendered separately in SVG.
- `dist/assets/fiscal-scene.webp`: treasury, workshop, bridge and infrastructure works in a coherent Youtopian landscape.
- `dist/assets/tax-scene.webp`: payroll desk, pay envelope, calculator, ledger and coin stacks.
- `dist/assets/goods-scene.webp`: coastal warning service, lighthouse and boats receiving its shared light.

Prompt direction for all four: crisp 16-bit pixel art, consistent navy/teal/green/coral/stone palette, no labels or policy outcomes rendered into the artwork, no yellow filter, no floating decorative objects. Map composition preserved the greybox geography and left areas for interactive game objects. Module scenes were requested as wide horizontal vignettes.

WebP encodes the generated scenes for practical web delivery. Silkscreen Regular and Bold are self-hosted under SIL OFL 1.1; the unmodified licence and official source URLs are in `dist/assets/fonts/`.
