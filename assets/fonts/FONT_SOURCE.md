# Silkscreen

Regular and Bold fonts from the official Google Fonts repository. Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the unmodified licence is included as `OFL.txt`.

- Repository: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/silkscreen
- Regular: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/silkscreen/Silkscreen-Regular.ttf
- Bold: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/silkscreen/Silkscreen-Bold.ttf
- Licence: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/silkscreen/OFL.txt

Retrieved 15 October 2026. Suitable for locally served headings and numbers; use a conventional text font for longer instructions.
